Banking Data Warehouse & Risk Analytics.

The Banking Data Warehouse is an end-to-end SQL project designed to handle the complex data engineering and analytical requirements of a modern financial institution. It focuses on the crucial steps required to ingest, clean, and analyze high-volume financial data to extract actionable business intelligence regarding customer credit risk, transaction volumetrics, and regional net-worth analysis.

The Story (The Process)

This project was built entirely from the ground up to showcase robust relational database architecture using SQL Server/MySQL syntax. The dataset simulates a realistic, fragmented banking environment—complete with over 10,000 transactions, 1,200 accounts, and 500 customers distributed globally.

Steps Taken in the Project:

Data Sourcing:

A highly realistic Python data generator was engineered to synthesize raw, fragmented datasets simulating a real-world banking environment. To accurately emulate production scenarios, intentional anomalies—such as missing contact information (NULLs) and incompatible currency tags—were strategically injected into the raw tables.

The Challenge (ETL & Cleansing):

The primary challenge was unifying the disjointed data entities (Customers, Accounts, Transactions). A rigorous ETL SQL script was written to sanitize the raw ingested files, standardize currency formats strictly to USD, enforce credit boundaries, and gracefully handle NULL values, preparing a pristine foundation for analytics.

The SQL Solution (Schema Design):

A resilient relational Star Schema was constructed. The architecture utilizes Customers and Accounts as the foundational Dimension tables, strictly enforcing Data Integrity via explicit Primary Key constraints. The highly volatile Transactions table serves as the Fact table, secured with resilient Foreign Key mappings to actively prevent orphaned financial records.

Core Concepts – Key Insights & Analytics:

  • High-Risk Clients (Credit Risk Scoring): Leveraging precise INNER JOIN logic and multi-condition WHERE clauses to instantly filter out high-risk profiles—customers holding active loan debts but suffering from sub-prime credit scores (< 600). The query surfaces the most severe negative debt exposures to the top.
  • Transactional Volumetrics: Utilizing aggregations (SUM, COUNT, AVG) coupled with robust GROUP BY structural grouping to calculate the sheer macroeconomic volume and liquidity flowing through specific account types (Checking vs. Savings).
  • Regional Financial Performance: Deploying advanced Common Table Expressions (WITH CTE) to pre-aggregate customer balances, followed by strict regional filtering (IN ('North America', 'Europe')) and dynamic sorting to isolate the Top 10 Elite High Net Worth Individuals (HNWI).
