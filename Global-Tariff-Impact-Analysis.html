Global Tariff Impact Analysis.

A high-level executive summary of how global trade tariffs affect market stability and sector vulnerability. This interactive strategic dashboard synthesizes massive flows of trade and damage variables into an actionable command center, enabling risk analysts to monitor financial disruption scenarios resulting from shifting trade agreements.

Data Engineering Narrative (The Kaggle Challenge)

The core success of this dashboard relies heavily on the Data Wrangling and engineering phase. The raw dataset was explicitly sourced from Kaggle. However, the initial data was completely unstructured, messy, and filled with inconsistent formats. What followed was a rigorous data integrity operation to build a clean pipeline for the dashboard.

Technical Actions (The Battle):

  • Data Cleanse: Standardized messy column names, corrected errant data types, and handled missing values that would otherwise break real-time charting.
  • Structural Anomalies: Corrected misaligned formats, structural anomalies in value types, and merged disjointed date strings into uniform analytical timestamps.
  • Feature Engineering: Constructed the 'Baseline vs. Impact' scenario logic from scratch, creating new fields that directly calculate estimated absolute and percentage-based revenue losses.
  • Geographic Precision: Deep-cleaned and standardized all geographic country and region names to guarantee 100% accurate rendering on the neon Plotly geographic Map.

Steps Taken in the Project:

  • Extraction & Integrity: Journeying from 'Raw & Messy Kaggle Data' by utilizing Pandas to sanitize and rebuild a structurally sound tabular dataset.
  • Hierarchical Visualization: Developing complex Sunburst and Treemap visualizations to unpack sector vulnerability.
  • Geopolitical Risk Plotting: Encoding latitude and longitude onto a 3D Earth vector map using Plotly Graph Objects to visualize global risk exposures.
  • Dashboard Assembly: Structuring the Streamlit layout with an absolute positioning CSS logic to forge a polished, 'Strategic Cockpit' interface.

Core Concepts:

  • Hierarchical Data Modeling (Sunburst): Breaking down broad market regions into specific granular product sector vulnerabilities.
  • Trend Projection: Utilizing comparative logic to map the 2018 historical baseline against a forecasted 2025 Tariff impact scenario.
  • Geopolitical Risk Assessment: Quantifying 'Financial Damage' and risk status through integrated KPI cards reflecting immediate threat scopes.
